quiet kid, the athlete, the class clown) and he ends up grinning with the same tray.

## 11. Principles to reuse / things not to copy
**Reuse:** give a list format a small arc (alone, then belonging) with bookend shots that differ only in the face; a
point-of-view camera flight as the way into the list; types told by costume and face; a call to action built into
the set as a sign; four beats per item.
**Don't copy:** these friends, the meme photo of a real person (don't put real people's photos in a set without
rights), the track.
