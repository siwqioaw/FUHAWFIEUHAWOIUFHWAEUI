## In one line
"Bro, imagine if a gold digger owned Roblox": you log in, an update says "Rich Players Only!", nobody can see you, so
you buy one gold suit and girls hug you, chase you and fight over you, until your Robux run out, everyone vanishes and
a popup says "LOL LOSER".

## Why it works
1. **A premise about the viewer's own game, pictured in under a second** (0.00-0.75): the title and the first line say
   the same thing, and a 0.18 s camera swoop lands on a girl with green dollar-sign eyes dancing in a room of cash
   while bills rain from the ceiling. The music starts on that landing (about 0.72). *heard + seen + measured*
2. **Three in-world "system message" popups as act breaks**: "UPDATE 1 / Rich Players Only!🤑" (4.5-6.0, the rule),
   "NICE! / Relationship Perks UNLOCKED!💕" (12.6-14.3, the reward), "LOL LOSER🤣 / Relationship Status Has Expired"
   (21.75-23.38, the punishment). Same design every time, so the viewer learns the format and the third one is the
   punchline. Each is read aloud in quotes and sits next to a bass boom. *seen + heard + measured*
3. **Every line is acted out literally**: "log in" = the narrator pops into an empty baseplate (3.25); "basically a
   ghost" = a rich player walks straight through him (7.55-8.6); "one expensive outfit" = a gold tux and a glow
   (9.0-9.5); "everybody disappears" = a tumbleweed rolls across an empty plaza (19.9-20.8). *seen*
4. **An escalation ladder, then a fast fall**: talk to you (10.6) → hugs and hearts (12.6) → chase (14.3) → "are you
   single" (16.0) → tug-of-war over you (17.2), a new rung every 1.2-2.0 s; then the gold suit strobes off in 0.57 s
   (18.65-19.22) and the world empties. Mirror lines: "suddenly everybody wants to talk to you" / "suddenly everybody
   disappears". *seen + heard*
5. **"You" in the script and the camera in your place**: second person throughout ("you log in", "you buy", "girls
   are following you"), and four of 14 shots sit behind the narrator or at his eyes (10.57, 12.57, 16.00, 19.88). The
   viewer is the poor player. *heard + seen*
6. **A real Roblox social truth under the joke** (interpretation, *guess*): plain-looking players get ignored and flashy
   avatars get attention. The video maps the gold-digger stereotype onto Robux, so no one needs it explained.

Structure: premise, rule, consequence, turn, a five-rung rise, a fall in under 3 s, a final notice. The arc is "poor
to rich to poor" in 20 s with affection tied to money at every step. The three popups open the first act, mark the
peak and close the video. The owner (the gold digger) is never seen after 4.6 s: she rules through her popups.
No explicit loop: it ends on the third popup and a boom cut off by the end (23.38); the restart "BRO" reads as a
reaction to it.

What is parodied: a game's update notice ("UPDATE 1"); achievement and unlock popups ("NICE! ... UNLOCKED!"); a
subscription expiring or a kick message ("LOL LOSER ... Has Expired"); the Robux economy and avatar flexing (an
expensive outfit = status; "rich players only" = paywalled access); dating culture in Roblox hangout games. Not
parodied: Roblox's website, app, store, login or logo; no fake Roblox screens appear (section 7).

Writing voice: second person, present tense, a run-on chain of clauses joined by "So", "And suddenly", "Then",
"Even"; sentences of 3-10 words; "suddenly" three times as a rhythm word; social life told in game jargon (update,
perks, unlocked, expired). The three popup lines are 3-5 words, deadpan, quoted.

## 11. Principles to reuse / things not to copy
**Reuse:**
- "If [a type] owned [a world the viewer knows]": the owner is a stereotype, and the world's rules change through
  in-world notices. Say the premise in one line and picture it within a second.
- The arc: rule, consequence, turn, a rising ladder of 4-5 rungs (each a bigger version of the same thing, 1.2-2 s
  each, the hero's face souring as it grows), a fall in under a second, then a final notice.
- Three system messages in one consistent design as act breaks; read each aloud in quotes; caption them lowercase in
  quotes; make the third one the punchline.
- Act out every figure of speech literally (ghost = walked through; everybody disappears = an empty plaza and a
  tumbleweed).
- Second-person narration with the camera behind the hero or at his eyes for the "you" lines.
- Hide swaps (costume, face) behind a turn or an occluding body; reveal with a spin and a glow.
- ECU first, then a 0.1-0.25 s pull-back to show the situation; push-ins for reactions; swoops to link framings.
- Build popups and signs as 3D props and move the camera to them, so they read as "in the game" rather than an edit,
  in a design of our own.
- Sound punctuation: start the music on the first reveal, not on frame 1; a 0.2-0.3 s dead stop before a reveal; a
  sub-bass boom right after a key line, with the cut landing inside it.
- One caption per line, changing on the cut; stressed words in caps; one trailing emoji; block centred about 77% down.
- A call-to-action sign built into the set, in our own words.
- Keep any strobe under 3 flashes a second (this one runs at 7.5 and fails WCAG 2.3.1).

**Don't copy:**
- This script and its jokes: the gold-digger premise, "UPDATE 1 / Rich Players Only!", "NICE! / Relationship Perks
  UNLOCKED!", "LOL LOSER / Relationship Status Has Expired", "basically a GHOST", the "suddenly everybody" mirror
  lines.
- The characters and looks: the narrator (fringe hair, purple round glasses, black jacket over a purple motorbike
  tee), the dollar-eyed blonde, the cat girls, the spiky rich player, the gold tux.
- The channel's markers: the "BRO" opener and pose, 🥀 and 😭🙏, the "subscribe pls🥀" board and its image.
- The billboard photos of real people, the ROBLOX logo tee and the "R$" bills.
- The popup layout that echoes Roblox's own prompt (dark box, title, rule, light "OK" button).
