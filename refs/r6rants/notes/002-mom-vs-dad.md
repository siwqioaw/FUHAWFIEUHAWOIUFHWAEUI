## 11. Principles to reuse / things not to copy
**Reuse:** a two-part comparison with one line of header text per half; the second half escalates and absurdly
outdoes the first; the music switch as the turn; one character who never changes face as a running joke; shots on
the beat (about 3 beats each); live "photo" tableaux with a small camera move; a reaction shot to close and loop;
under 12 s. A format that needs no voice at all is cheap to make and travels across languages.
**Don't copy:** these particular photos (noob pile, war, biker with the goth girl), the parents' and kid's looks, the
songs.
