## In one line
The same sunglasses narrator as 001 asks "what if your mom owned Roblox?", then acts out three escalating answers
inside a game. A screen-time warning appears the moment you spawn. Your character freezes mid-fight because dinner's
ready, so you get beaten up. Then mom joins your server and yells about the mess in your virtual house.

## Why it works
1. **A premise every Roblox kid can picture in 2 s** (0.00-2.12): "Bro, what if your mom owned Roblox?" The person who
   already controls their screen time now controls the game itself. *heard*
2. **Real parent behaviour turned literally into game mechanics, escalating**. First a rule (screen-time warning,
   4.13-6.13). Then the rule enforced at a cost: you freeze mid-chase and get jumped (10.57-13.93). Then an invasion:
   she joins your game and nags about your in-game house (13.93-18.38). *heard + seen*
3. **The rules are props in the world, not edit graphics.** The two "system" pop-ups are dark panels built in the 3D
   scene: one rises out of the ground (4.13-5.2), one scales into the sky (11.48-11.57). They look like part of the game,
   so the premise feels real. *seen*
4. **A refrain closes each gag**: "Bro, I just got here!" (5.88), "Bro, I'm getting jumped!" (12.48), "Bro, it's a
   virtual house!" (18.24). Each comes with a new reaction face. The viewer learns the rhythm by the second one.
   *heard + seen*
5. **Game logic as visual jokes**: logging in shown as a spawn pop-in (2.58), joining shown as mom popping into a
   corridor (14.08), the freeze shown as a blank face on a mid-stride pose (10.57), and an impact flash on the beating
   (13.45). *seen*
6. **It loops, and it rewards rewatching.** It ends on "BRO... It's a VIRTUAL house" and starts on "Bro, what if...". A
   hidden screaming-face extra sits in three places: the window at 0.57, the cliff top at 3.3-6.5, the kitchen table
   at 15.1-20.5. *seen + heard*

## 10. Packaging
- **Title:** "If Your Mom OWNED Roblox😭🙏". The format phrase "If [person] OWNED Roblox" with the verb in caps, 😭🙏, and no
  hashtags. It is the same title formula as 007, "If A Gold Digger OWNED Roblox😭🙏".
- **First frame:** the narrator alone with "BRO" (consistent channel branding). It is a weak thumbnail. The two-shot

## 11. Principles to reuse / things not to copy
**Reuse:**
- The "If [person] OWNED [the game]" hypothetical: pick someone whose real-world power over kids gets absurd as
  platform rules.
- Turn each of their real behaviours into a literal game mechanic, and escalate: rule, then enforcement with a cost,
  then invasion of your space.
- Show the rules as props inside the world, built in Studio (a sign rising from the ground, a panel growing in the
  sky), not as edit overlays.
- Spawning or joining shown as an empty frame, a pop-in and a camera rush.
- A freeze shown as a blank face on a locked mid-action pose.
- Close every gag with the same refrain line from the narrator.
- Warm home sets against a saturated game world; one house map can serve as both the real and the in-game home.
- An impact flash and camera jolt synced to a boom.
- A hidden recurring extra as rewatch bait.
- Cut just before the content word of each line.

**Don't copy:**
- This script and its jokes: the 30-minute screen-time warning, "Times up, Dinner's ready", getting jumped while
  frozen, "it's a virtual house".
- Mom's design: red spiky bun on classic yellow.
- The narrator's look and his "bro" refrain.
- The screaming-face extra.
- The panel texts and emoji.
- Above all, anything that apes Roblox's own UI. The dark Gotham-style prompt with a title, a rule and a full-width
  button sits close to Roblox's kick dialog. Design our own panel style.

## 12. Checks against what we learned on 001
- **Inflated cut list:** holds (24 raw against 12 hard cuts plus 1 jump). Here the inflation comes from camera rushes
  and orbits, two character pop-ins, a freeze pose snap and one false positive (11.92) more than from snappy pose
  animation. Clusters: 0.52-0.63, 2.40-2.72, 6.13-6.50, 18.38-18.43. The detector caught every real cut, and did not
  flag the 13.45 jolt (not a cut).
- **"Misses fast swoops":** mostly the reverse here. Most camera moves of 0.05-0.15 s were flagged as cuts, some of
  them two or three times (0.52/0.63, 6.27/6.32/6.37). Only the opening push-in (0.00-0.27) and the last pull-back
  (18.85-19.00) went unflagged. Count by eye either way.
- **Faces swapped on the word, no lip sync:** holds. About 15 states in 20.5 s, slightly fewer per second than 001's
  25 in 28 s. One face texture (dazed) is reused for two meanings: spawn and freeze.
- **Captions:** the same style as 001 (font, weight, outline, caps on stressed words, 🥀 as the default emoji),
  centred about 76.5% from the top.
- **Bass hits after punchlines:** holds (five hits), and the gap level is above the voice as in 001. Because the voice
  has no real pauses, the hits sit under the edges of words rather than in silence.
- **Things 001 did not have:** graphics inside the scene that carry the premise (two in-world panels); a world change
  (home to game); a narrator who plays "you" rather than reporting on someone else. As in 001, the edit adds nothing
  but captions.
