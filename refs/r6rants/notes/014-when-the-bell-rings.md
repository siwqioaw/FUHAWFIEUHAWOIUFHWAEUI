## In one line
The teacher checks her watch, the bell's about to ring, and here's what every kind of kid does when it does: the door
runner, the one who was asleep, the nerd reminding everyone about homework, and me, sprinting out grinning.

## Why it works
1. **A shared moment everyone has lived:** the end-of-class bell. The header says it all from frame 0. *seen*
2. **A list of recognisable types,** each named by a coloured label and acted in one shot: viewers tag friends
   ("that's you"). *seen*
3. **"Me:" as the last item** turns the list into a joke about the viewer: the narrator is the most excited of all
   (9.63). *seen*
4. **Locked to the music:** every type gets exactly four beats. *measured*
5. **11.6 s**, so it loops several times; the end (running out of the door) cuts back to the teacher's watch. *seen*

## 11. Principles to reuse / things not to copy
**Reuse:** a shared everyday moment as the header; a list of 3-4 labelled types, one shot and four beats each; the
narrator ("Me:") as the last type; coloured labels under a white header; a recurring kid the audience knows; first
and last shots that rhyme; about 11 s, music only.
**Don't copy:** these particular types and gags, the hedgehog-like character (another company's character), the meme
cat photo, the track.
