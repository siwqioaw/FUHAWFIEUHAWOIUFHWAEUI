# Mini-drama (family B)

**Use when** the concept is an odd habit with a hidden reason ("He ordered two dinners every night").

**Length** 33-37 s; the story engine without a stated rule: the odd habit shown (0-15%), the people who judge it
(15-35%), the habit continues as something changes (35-60%), the reveal of the reason (75-90%), the last image answers
the first. One warm storyteller, lo-fi or piano bed, heartbreak stings on the emotional peaks, one real-world sound
for the reveal (knocks, a door). Emotional stories hit 1 of 4: give it a twist the comments will argue about.
