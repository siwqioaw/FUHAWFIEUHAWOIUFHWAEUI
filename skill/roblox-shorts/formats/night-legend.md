# Night legend (family B, spooky)

**Use when** the concept is a town rule or warning with a scary reason ("Nobody goes outside at night").

**Length** 30-35 s, the story engine of `in-this-world.md` with a warning in place of the rule. Night lighting
(`street` set with `{"night": True}`: black sky, faces lit like day), a dark pad bed.

## The bounds (teen, spooky but safe)

- Harm implied, never shown: a dropped object, a door left open, mud, never blood. Victims stay alive.
- Every scary image under about 2 s; drawn cartoon faces for the scares (`scared`, `shock`, `evil`).
- End on a chase, a plea or a reveal, never a catch. Put a heart under the fear (protecting a sibling).

## Beats

The warning (0-15%); the reason, half-told (15-30%); the hero breaks the rule for someone else (30-50%); three clues,
escalating (50-80%); the truth, re-reading a clue (85%); the last image: the thing, gone or watching, on the last word.

## Rubric

A warning in the first sentence; three clues; nothing graphic; the scariest image under 2 s; a heart in it.
