# "If [type] OWNED Roblox" (family A)

**Use when** a type's habits would make funny server rules (a mom, a little brother, a teacher, a gold digger). 2 of
2 passed 1M views.

**Length** 20-24 s; 66-73 words; second person ("You log in..."); a new framing every 1.6-1.7 s. 60 fps.

## Beat table

| Time | Beat | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.0 | the premise | the narrator (or "you") logs in; the type appears | "If [type] owned Roblox..." |
| 2-8 | rule 1 | an in-world notice panel rises into the scene with the rule; the rule enforced | a ding on the notice; boom on the cost |
| 8-14 | rule 2 | bigger cost | a cha-ching or error tone on the notice |
| 14-20 | rule 3 | invades your space; the refrain ("I just got here!") | boom |
| last 2 s | a rise then a fast fall | the worst notice; cut | final boom |

**Notices** are props built in the world: a dark panel with our own shape, colour and font (never Roblox's kick-prompt
layout). Build one with `kit.Set.sign` on a part placed in the shot (`building.md`), and move the camera to meet it.
Quote the rule in lowercase on the panel.

## Rubric

A rule per beat, each enforced at a cost; a refrain; notices that are our own design; no Roblox logo or UI; ends on
the worst rule.
