## "In this world" stories (family B)

### 1. The siren (22)

**In this world, a siren goes off over your head when you lie.** Nobody can lie, so nobody can keep a secret: no
surprise parties, ever. Zoe's best friend has been "busy" every Saturday for a month, and the siren never went off.
Zoe decides she has been replaced. The twist: it was true. She was busy, planning Zoe's surprise party, the first
one in years ("Technically true.").

- *Shown in 2 s:* a 🚨 over a liar's head and a siren.
- *Benefit becomes cost:* no lies means no surprises; the loophole (the literal truth) is the twist.
- *Loophole for the comments:* do white lies count? What about sarcasm?
- *Quotable:* "Technically true."
- *Sets:* hallway, living room (the party), street. *Cast:* bestie, me, extras. *Icons:* 🚨, 🎉.
- *Built:* `balloons` and `cake` props, the `siren` effect; the banner is a `sign` with "SURPRISE!".

### 2. The battery (22)

**In this world, your phone loses 1% every time you lie.** Everyone's phone is dead by lunch. Except Ava's: 100%,
every day, so everyone calls her the most honest girl in school. Sam finds out why: she hasn't talked to anyone in
weeks. He sits with her anyway. "I'm only here because I'm bored." His phone: 99%.

- *Shown in 2 s:* a 📱 and a battery number dropping over the liar.
- *Benefit becomes cost:* the honest girl is the loneliest; the hero's lie is the kind one.
- *Loophole:* does it charge back when you tell the truth?
- *Quotable:* "That's a one-percent lie."
- *Sets:* bedroom, cafeteria (lunch), hallway. *Cast:* me, bestie, popular_girl, extras. *Icons:* 📱.
- *To build:* nothing: a number over a head is an overlay with text (`{"text": "99%"}`, `docs/MAKING.md`).

### 3. The crown (21)

**In this world, a crown floats over the most liked person in every room.** The popular girl has worn it in every
room for three years, and she would do anything to keep it. Then it starts to flicker. Every time she looks away it
drifts to the quiet kid in the back, the one who lends everyone a pencil. The last room: her little sister's
bedroom, where it has always been hers, and always will be.

- *Shown in 2 s:* a 👑 over a head as a room turns to look.
- *Benefit becomes cost:* the crown is a job she can't quit.
- *Loophole:* what if two people tie?
- *Quotable:* "It's not even real gold."
- *Sets:* classroom, hallway, bedroom. *Cast:* popular_girl, nerd, little_brother as the sibling (or a new look),
  extras. *Icons:* 👑, ✨.
- *To build:* nothing; the crown is an overlay.

### 4. The receipts (21)

**In this world, the last text you sent floats over your head for an hour.** Everyone walks around with their last
text showing. The scariest senior's says "goodnight mom ❤". The new kid's says "what do I even say to him". Then she
walks past Sam, and he reads it on her.

- *Shown in 2 s:* a 💬 with a line of text over a head.
- *Benefit becomes cost:* nothing private; the payoff is that it shows who someone really is.
- *Loophole:* what if you never text anyone?
- *Quotable:* "Read it. Out loud."
- *Sets:* hallway, classroom. *Cast:* me, bestie, jock (the senior), extras.
- *To build:* nothing: a 💬 overlay with its text (`{"text": "goodnight mom"}`), sized so the line fits the bubble.

## Rants (family A)

### 5. The friend who's "five minutes away" (21)

**"Nah, why does every friend group have THAT friend who's always five minutes away?"** "Five minutes away" (still
in bed). "Pulling up" (in the shower). "I'm outside" (they are not outside). They arrive as everyone is leaving,
holding a smoothie, for themselves.

- *Quotable:* "Pulling up." · *Punch:* the smoothie, a boom.
- *Sets:* street, bedroom. *Cast:* narrator, rich_kid (the late friend), extras. *Icons:* 📱, 💤.
- *Built:* the `smoothie` prop.

### 6. The sibling bathroom (19)

**"Why does your older sister ALWAYS need the bathroom the second you do?"** Forty-five minutes. "Two more minutes."
Music through the door. She comes out and says you take too long.

- *Quotable:* "Two more minutes." · *Punch:* her line, a thud.
- *Sets:* living room (the bathroom door is the set's door). *Cast:* narrator, bestie as the sister. *Icons:* 💢.
- *To build:* nothing.

### 7. The "this will be on the test" teacher (18)

**"Why does every teacher say 'this will be on the test' about EVERYTHING?"** The date: on the test. Their dog's
name: on the test. The test: one question, the one thing they never said.

- *Quotable:* "On the test." · *Sets:* classroom (the board carries the joke). *Cast:* narrator, teacher, extras.

## "If X owned Roblox" (family A)

### 8. The school nurse (21)

**"If the school nurse OWNED Roblox."** Every injury gets an ice pack. Lag? Drink some water. Fell in the lava? Ice
pack. Deleted your account? Have you tried lying down? Every rule appears as an in-world notice, signed "the nurse".

- *Quotable:* "Have you had water today?" · *Punch:* the server shutting down, and an ice pack.
- *Sets:* classroom as the nurse's office, stage_void for the notices. *Cast:* mom's look as the nurse, me, extras.
  *Icons:* ❤‍🩹, ⚠.
- *Built:* the `ice_pack` prop; the cot is the bedroom's bed, the notices `sign`s.

### 9. The gym teacher (20)

**"If a gym teacher OWNED Roblox."** Twenty push-ups before every obby. No walking, running only. "The floor is lava"
is the warm-up. Respawning costs a lap.

- *Quotable:* "That's a lap." · *Sets:* gym, street. *Cast:* jock as the
  teacher, nerd, extras. *Icons:* 🔥, 💦.
- *Built:* the `whistle` effect, the `gym` set (a court with its lines, hoops, bleachers).

## Night legends (family B, spooky but safe)

### 12. The hallway phone (19)

**"Never answer the hallway phone after the last bell."** It rings in the empty school. Every kid knows the rule.
Sam stays late, and it rings, and rings. He answers. A tired voice: the janitor, asking him to please go home,
because he wants to lock up. Harm only implied, the scare a misdirection, every frame under 2 s.

- *Sets:* hallway with the `night` or `eerie` lighting. *Cast:* me, extras. *Icons:* 📱, 👀.
- *Built:* the `wall_phone` prop.
