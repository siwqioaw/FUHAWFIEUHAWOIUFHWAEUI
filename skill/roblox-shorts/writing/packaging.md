# Packaging: title, description, hashtags

Write `videos/<slug>/package.md`. The owner pastes it when they upload; you never upload.

## Titles (under 60 characters, the premise, one word in caps)

| Format | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| rant | Why does every [thing] have THAT [one] | Why does every group project have THAT one person |
| owned | If [type] OWNED Roblox | If Your Little Brother OWNED Roblox |
| in this world | In this world, [rule in plain words] | In this world, you hear a DING when someone talks about you |
| comparison | [A] VS [B] | First day of school VS Last day of school |
| types list | When [moment] / Types of [group] | When the teacher says "pick a partner" |

No clickbait the video doesn't pay off; no kid-coded words; "Roblox" only as a descriptive word, never as if
official.

## Description

Two lines: the hook restated as a question to answer in the comments (the loophole), then a credit line for every
asset that needs one (from `ATTRIBUTION.md`) and: "Not affiliated with Roblox."

## Hashtags

3-5: `#roblox #robloxanimation #shorts` plus one for the topic (`#school`, `#siblings`, `#story`).
